### **SCENE 1 (HOOK)**

Location: Countess' terrace, late morning — white stone floors, milk rosé bottles, untouched pastries, lilies, bright Beverly Hills sunlight

Characters: Purrlita, Countess Catastrophe, Kitty LePurr, Dominique de Claw, Bambi von Fluffhauser

Emotional Beat: absurd shock → intrigued tension

Function: Cold open hook; establishes tone, humor, and chaos level

DIALOGUE:

PURRLITA
 "Your daughter got knocked up by a parakeet… and you're fine with that?"

(Reaction cuts.)

PURRLITA
 "…oh that's dark."

END SCENE

---

### **SCENE 2 (INTRODUCTIONS + POWER SETUP)**

Location: Same terrace, continuous — Dominique enters, Bambi brought to table, milk rosé being poured

Characters: Dominique de Claw, Countess Catastrophe, Kitty LePurr, Purrlita, Bambi von Fluffhauser

Emotional Beat: composed introduction → layered social judgment

Power Shift: Dominique introduces Bambi → group collectively establishes dominance

Function: Introduces all characters, defines hierarchy, positions Bambi as outsider

DIALOGUE:

DOMINIQUE
 "Ladies… meet the newest purrmanent resident of Beverly Hills."

(beat)

"Bambi, meet… your future problems."

BAMBI
 "Hi!! I'm so excited to be here—"

DOMINIQUE
 "Don't."

DOMINIQUE
 "That's Kitty. Former actress… currently selective."

KITTY
 "I don't chase roles. Roles find me… eventually."

DOMINIQUE
 "Countess Catastrophe. Old money, new problems."

COUNTESS
 "I prefer 'mismanaged headlines.'"

DOMINIQUE
 "Purrlita. No one knows what she does."

PURRLITA
 "I'm in events."

(beat)

"Emotionally."

DOMINIQUE
 "And you know me—Upper East Side transplant…"

(beat)

"…with a lot of money."

PURRLITA (clocking Bambi)
 "Oh I like her."

(leans in)

"She looks refundable."

END SCENE

---

### **SCENE 3 (CONFESSIONAL — KITTY)**

Location: Confessional set — warm neutral tones, soft glam lighting, milk rosé glass in hand

Characters: Kitty LePurr

Emotional Beat: polite commentary → cutting social rule

Function: Explains Beverly Hills social code to audience; defines tone of judgment

DIALOGUE:

KITTY
 "In Beverly Hills, we don't judge…"

(beat)

"…we just notice."

(leans in slightly)

"And we ALL notice that that outfit is screaming Panhandle."

END SCENE

---

### **SCENE 4 (TABLE DYNAMIC + CLIFFHANGER)**

Location: Countess' terrace, slightly later — drinks flowing, tighter framing, tension settled over table

Characters: Dominique de Claw, Kitty LePurr, Countess Catastrophe, Purrlita, Bambi von Fluffhauser

Emotional Beat: surface politeness → social freeze

Power Shift: Bambi attempts connection → Countess reasserts dominance

Function: Establishes group worldview, isolates Bambi, ends on tension hook

DIALOGUE:

BAMBI
 "I just think it's so amazing how you've all built your lives…"

DOMINIQUE
 "No one builds anything here. We acquire."

PURRLITA
 "Or inherit."

COUNTESS
 "Or recover."

PURRLITA
 "Or lie."

BAMBI
 "…what do you do?"

(Silence.)

(Countess slowly raises one eyebrow.)

END SCENE

---

### **SCENE 5 (SEASON TEASER)**

Location: Various — fast montage (interiors, night, shadows, confessionals)

Characters: All

Emotional Beat: rising chaos

Function: Hooks audience into future episodes; previews conflict arcs

DIALOGUE:

PURRLITA
 "You smell like store-brand tuna."

DOMINIQUE
 "I always tell the truth."

(beat)

"Eventually."

BAMBI
 "I thought this was a sisterhood!"

COUNTESS
 "In Monaco, this wouldn't even register."

KITTY
 "You don't get to judge me."

PURRLITA (whisper)
 "Oh… this is going to ruin her."

END SCENE
